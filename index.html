Frequently Asked Questions

How long does iGenics take to work?

Most positive reviews mention a 4 to 12 week window before noticeable changes. One user required about four months to feel depth perception improvements. Consistency matters more than dosage timing.

Is iGenics safe for seniors on medication?

iGenics is generally well-tolerated, but cardiac patients on blood thinners should consult their doctor first. Ginkgo biloba and turmeric may interact with anticoagulant medications.

Can iGenics replace my glasses?

No. iGenics is a nutritional supplement, not a vision correction tool. Users consistently report that reading glasses are still necessary for fine print.
